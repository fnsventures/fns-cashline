# FNS Cashline — Business Guide

F & S Ventures · Cash franchise operations · Bishnupriya Fuels, Padmanavpur

---

## 1. How the model works

```
You (franchisee)                    Franchise partner (bank / WLA)
       │                                      │
       │  Load cash into ATM                  │
       ├─────────────────────────────────────►│
       │                                      │ Customers withdraw
       │  Commission per transaction          │
       │◄─────────────────────────────────────┤
       │                                      │
       │  Unused cash swept to your bank      │
       │◄─────────────────────────────────────┤
```

**Your revenue** = commission on withdrawals (+ optional space rent if negotiated separately).

**Your capital at risk** = cash sitting inside the ATM until it is swept back.

---

## 2. Before you sign

| Item | Ask the partner |
|------|-----------------|
| Machine type | White Label ATM (WLA), brown label, or bank-owned |
| Minimum float | How much cash you must keep loaded |
| Commission | Per txn rate, caps, and payment cycle (weekly / monthly) |
| TDS | Whether they deduct TDS on commission (usually 10% u/s 194H) |
| Cash settlement | Sweep frequency and bank account |
| Insurance | Who covers theft / vandalism |
| Electricity & space | Who pays; monthly rent if any |
| SLA | Uptime, refill alerts, cassette swap support |
| Agreement term | Lock-in, exit clause, machine removal cost |

---

## 3. KPIs to track in this app

| KPI | Formula / source |
|-----|------------------|
| **Cash deployed** | Sum(cash loads) − Sum(cash settlements) |
| **Commission / txn** | Net commission ÷ transaction count (from settlement) |
| **Monthly yield** | Net commission ÷ average cash deployed |
| **Load frequency** | Days between refills (from cash load dates) |

---

## 4. Suggested commission fields

When recording a commission settlement:

- **Transaction count** — from partner statement
- **Gross commission** — before TDS
- **TDS** — tax deducted at source (if any)
- **Net commission** — amount credited to your bank
- **Reference** — UTR / settlement ID from partner

---

## 5. Tax notes (not legal advice)

- Commission income is taxable business income.
- TDS credit appears on Form 26AS; reconcile with settlements.
- GST generally does not apply to ATM franchise commission (confirm with your CA).
- Maintain partner statements + this app’s records for ITR and audit.
