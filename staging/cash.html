Night inquiry → ATM load
  1. Night — record ATM inquiry (closing balance + inquiry slip).
  2. Morning — load the dispensed amount into the ATM (load receipt).

Dispensed = opening balance − closing balance. Load amount is locked for operators. Admins can override the load amount on an open inquiry, or top up without an inquiry when cash in ATM is below capital.
